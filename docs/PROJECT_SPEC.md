# Bangladesh Restaurant — Full-Stack Web Application

## Business Configuration

Country: Bangladesh
Currency: BDT (৳)
Language: English only
Restaurant Admin: Super Admin
Customer Types: Local + International

## Payment Methods

Local:
- Cash
- Cash on Delivery
- bKash
- Nagad
- QR Payment
- Bank Transfer

International:
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express

Final payment gateway selection will be confirmed against merchant eligibility,
supported channels, fees, settlement, and production account requirements.
